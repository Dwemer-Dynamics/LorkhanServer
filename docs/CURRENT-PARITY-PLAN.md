## Supported differences and boundaries

Retain Lorkhan gold branding and supported Herika page structure. Keep Background Life,
AI Quest Manager/Active Quests, Soulgaze, ITT and server plugins excluded. Skyrim physics-held
items, scripted inn rental and follower home/membership require their own faithful Morrowind
semantics; inventory removal or ordinary Rest must not be mislabeled as those features.

Director requires an explicitly selected dedicated connector; no hidden standard-connector
fallback. Narrator physical actions require an observed executor and both policies. Spell success
means release, not a guaranteed hit. Service success means menu opened, not a purchase.

No arbitrary model console, Lua, MWScript, SQL, paths, URLs or general record creation. The existing
physical-diary exception remains diary-only. Preserve credentials, voices, settings and data;
keep the GitHub workflow disabled. User-run in-game acceptance remains explicitly unverified
and is separate from this automated build/deployment goal.
